{%- comment -%} Shows an event time. A time typed as just "18:00" is read by the site's YAML reader as a number of seconds (64800), so this turns such numbers back into hours and minutes. {%- endcomment -%} {%- assign raw = include.time -%} {%- assign probe = raw | jsonify -%} {%- if probe contains '"' -%} {{- raw -}} {%- else -%} {%- assign hours = raw | divided_by: 3600 -%} {%- assign minutes = raw | modulo: 3600 | divided_by: 60 -%} {%- if minutes < 10 %}{% assign minutes = minutes | prepend: '0' %}{% endif -%} {{- hours }}:{{ minutes -}} {%- endif -%}
