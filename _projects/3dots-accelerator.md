Launched with the aim of building a thriving cultural entrepreneurship ecosystem, **3dots Accelerator_001** was a dedicated acceleration program that supported innovative and responsible founders on a global scale. Centered around creativity, the program was carefully designed to address the unique needs of cultural entrepreneurs, recognizing that every founder’s journey was different.

As part of the program, our Istanbul bootcamp brought founders together in a setting shaped by shared insights and collective growth, with CoBAC WorkSpace providing an essential home for collaboration. The journey continued in Berlin, where we hosted our Demo Day at betahaus, an evening filled with inspiration, meaningful connections, and presentations from Bioground (fka En Küçük Müze) (Türkiye), Guayaba (France), and myCulture (Austria).

**We’re excited for what’s next, continuing our mission to support startups and drive change in the creative economy.**
