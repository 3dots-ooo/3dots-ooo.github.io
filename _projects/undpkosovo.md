**We were commissioned by UNDP Kosovo in 2025.**

The work includes **identifying funding opportunities**, **shaping the scope and structure of the Creative Industries Fund**, and **defining eligibility and selection criteria aligned with international best practices**. In collaboration with local experts, institutions, and UNDP teams, the project aims to unlock the full potential of Kosovo’s creative economy through practical and data-informed solutions.
