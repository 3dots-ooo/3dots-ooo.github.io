## Jazz, experimental & more

Each year, our programming team at Bozcaada Jazz Festival carefully curates a selection of fresh and contemporary music from all over the world. Since its foundation, the festival has hosted hundreds of musicians from all over the world.

Now the festival has 3 different stages. Together with pop up concerts around the island, festival hosts more than 50 music happenings apart from ‘Keşif’ gatherings.

Bozcaada Jazz Festival aims to promote today’s music centered around jazz and improvised music but not limited to it.

## BCF ‘Keşif’

Since its first year, Bozcaada Jazz Festival hosted educational gatherings, experience events such as gastronomy meet ups, panels, workshops, nature walks, well being events & more.

Each year, what we now call ‘Keşif’ events got bigger and naturally attracted more and more attendees both as professionals, or just curious minds. Our aim here is to partner up with change makers and teams that inspire us so that they inspire other people, our community.

Keşif is where entertainment meets inspiration. It’s now gotten to a point that has its own team and operation. We curate Keşif events around culture, design, entrepreneurship with a focus on the local culture.

One can rightfully ask, what is Keşif? It means ‘discovery’ in Turkish.
