On the morning of July 22, we came together for the very [**first Impact for Breakfast gathering in Istanbul**](https://impactforbreakfast.com/eventpage?eid=YsjMO9Nq).

We met and connected with investors, entrepreneurs, ecosystem stakeholders, service providers, and those curious about impact, in the inspiring setting of Barın Han, a place with a unique role in Istanbul’s cultural memory.

Following the Istanbul meeting, [**we met at the Bozcaada Jazz Festival**](https://impactforbreakfast.com/eventpage?eid=3OiFz1Y5) on September 6th.

In the island’s tranquil atmosphere, we engaged in encounters, new connections, and in-depth conversations about impact.
