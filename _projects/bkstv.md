## Bursa Feyha Çelenk Theatre Entrepreneurship Support Program

Co-developed with **Alethina Impact.**

Under the leadership of the **Bursa Culture, Art and Tourism Foundation** (BKSTV), we announced a new support model designed to sustain independent performing arts productions.

Named after **Feyha Çelenk**, a doyen of Bursa theater and one of the founding members of the foundation, this valuable initiative reflects her dedication to the arts, her hard work, and the inspiration she has provided to younger generations.

Through this support structure, co-developed by 3dots and Alethina Impact, we aim to contribute to solving the challenges faced by entrepreneurs in the performing arts and to create independent spaces for new productions. We are excited to transform our long-standing reflections on the sustainability of the culture and arts ecosystem, cultural financing, and innovative funding models into concrete, impact-driven structures. We believe this journey, full of learnings, is just the beginning.

We sincerely thank everyone whose paths crossed with ours and who contributed to bringing this vision to life. We are deeply grateful to **Emre Feza Soysal, Meral Yetil, Can Atacık, İrem Topçuoğlu,** and **Ali Durmuş Cevlan** for their invaluable contributions and support throughout this process. 🎭✨
