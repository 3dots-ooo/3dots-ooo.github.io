## Bozcaada Jazz Festival Impact Report, 2023 and 2025

First of its kind in Türkiye, our impact measurement for Bozcaada Jazz Festival shed light for festival’s long term strategy.

In 2023, we partnered up with Impact Hub Ankara & DNB Lab for taking a step towards measuring our positive and negative effects. We announced our report in 2024 which paved the way for our strategy in 2025.

On festival’s 9th year in 2025 as 3dots, we started working on the second chapter of Bozcaada Jazz Festival’s impact report with the support of our sponsor Paribu.
