From audiences to festivals, from producers to institutions — how can we strengthen the sustainability of jazz and improvised music within this wide ecosystem?

In this panel, bringing together public institutions, the private sector, and independent cultural initiatives, we will seek common ground on many topics — from audience engagement to creative production processes.
