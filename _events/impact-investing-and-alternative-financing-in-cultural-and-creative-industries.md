We are meeting on Friday, October 31st, for the “**Impact Investing and Alternative Financing in Cultural and Creative Industries**” event!

At this gathering, we will open a discussion on alternative financing models to increase the impact of the cultural and creative sectors on economic and social transformation. We will address how the field can be strengthened with sustainable financing models, the role of impact investing in this area, and the exciting potential of innovative collaborations.

**Speakers:** [Oktay Kargül](https://www.linkedin.com/in/oktay-karg%C3%BCl-0830ba120/), [Cagil Ozdemir](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cagilozdemir/), [Cem Leon Menase](https://www.linkedin.com/in/cemmenase/), [Elif Atmaca](https://www.linkedin.com/in/elifatmaca/), [Simten Birsoz Inanc](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simtenbirsozinanc/), [Ali Atakan Can](https://www.linkedin.com/in/ali-atakan-can-62305a146/), [Betül Selcen Özer](https://www.linkedin.com/in/bet%C3%BClselcen%C3%B6zer/), [Mustafa Gül, Ph.D.](https://www.linkedin.com/in/gulmustafa/)
